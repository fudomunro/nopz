Further separate the activity of validating whether a condition has been met from the activity of meeting that condition.

A condition is a regulation

Agents are beaurocrats responsible for confirming that a condition/regulation is being met.

Clerks are low-level beaurocrats that make changes.

Once their changes are complete, they ask a senior beaurocrat to validate that their change conforms to regulations.

Sometimes changes may include new or modified regulations, which must be accepted first by the senior beaurocrat before the other changes are validated.

Regulations provide boundaries on acceptable behavior.

Regulations could be:
 - Test coverage is 85%+
 - TypeCheckers pass
 -


Regulations ideally have a deterministic evaluations, so checking compliance on existing regulations is fast and consistent.

(Does a beaurocrat assess the regulations before work begins?)

When work has been validated, it can be merged.

If validation fails, a new clerk is assigned to fix the failure, and the cycle continues.
